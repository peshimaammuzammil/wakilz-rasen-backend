**Price:** Ask the site team for direct pricing quotes
**Handover:** Phase 1 — July 2027 | Phase 2 — August 2028
**Clubhouse:** 72,000 sq.ft

- Exclusive private corridors for ultimate luxury and privacy
- Very low density — just 98 units per acre (rare for Kokapet)
- Uninterrupted views of Kokapet Lake from upper floors
- Highest carpet area efficiency at 72%
- Impressive floor-to-ceiling height of 10.8 feet

Best for: Luxury buyers wanting premium 3 BHK or 4 BHK in Kokapet with green spaces
and lake views. Ideal for families who value low density and exclusivity.
